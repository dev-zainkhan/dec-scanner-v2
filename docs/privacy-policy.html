Scanova Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026  |  Last updated: October 13, 2026

Scanova ("the app", "we") is a document-scanning and PDF utility for Android. This policy explains what information is handled when you use the app, why, and the choices available to you.

Summary

  • Your scans, document names, and PDFs are processed and stored on your device. We do not upload them to any server, and we do not operate accounts.
  • The app shows ads using Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google may collect device and advertising information to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change that choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • You can delete your documents in the app at any time. Files you saved or shared outside the app must be deleted from their destination separately.

Information Handled by Scanova

Documents and files

When you scan a document, the app opens the camera through Google's on-device document scanner. Scanned page images, page details, and document names are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Scanning and all PDF tools (merge, split, compress, and lock) run on your device; the app does not upload your scans or imported PDFs.

When you choose a PDF using the system file picker, the app can access only the file you selected, and only to perform the operation you requested.

The app stores basic preferences (such as the library layout) and, if you save a PDF, the folder you selected for saving. These are stored on your device.

Passwords you enter in the Lock PDF tool are used only to encrypt that PDF on your device. They are not stored or sent anywhere.

Advertising (Google Mobile Ads)

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show banner and interstitial ads. According to Google's disclosure for this SDK, it may automatically collect and share:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate the general location of your device;
  • app interactions, such as app launches, taps, and ad views;
  • diagnostic information about app and SDK performance; and
  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID.

Google uses this information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention, and states that it is encrypted in transit. Google handles this information under its own policies. See Google's Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from apps that use its services, and Google's Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

Ad consent

If you are in a region where consent is required, the app shows a consent message provided by Google (the User Messaging Platform) the first time it starts. Depending on your choice, Google shows personalized or non-personalized ads, or, where required, limited ads. You can review or change your choice at any time from Settings → Ad privacy choices in the app.

You can also reset or delete your Android advertising ID in your device's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your device).

What we do not collect

We do not create accounts, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, or microphone audio. We do not use our own analytics or crash-reporting services.

How Information Is Shared

We do not receive or share your documents. Advertising-related information is collected directly by Google as described above.

If you use Share, Android opens a sharing screen so you can choose a recipient or app. If you use Save to Phone, the PDF is saved to the folder you choose. The recipients, apps, and storage providers you choose handle files under their own policies.

Permissions

  • Camera: used by Google's document scanner only while you are scanning.
  • Selected files: PDFs you pick through the system file picker, for the operation you requested.
  • Selected save folder: when you first save a PDF, Android asks you to pick a folder. The app remembers it for later saves. You can choose a different folder at any time, and revoke access in Android settings.
  • Internet and advertising ID: used by Google Mobile Ads to load and measure ads.

Retention and Deletion

Your documents and preferences stay on your device until you delete them in the app or uninstall the app. To delete a document, open it and tap the delete button. Uninstalling the app removes its private data from your device.

PDFs you saved to a folder or shared with another app are separate copies. Delete them with your file manager or in the app you shared them to.

Google controls how long it keeps information collected through its advertising services. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

Security

The app keeps working documents in private app storage provided by Android and processes them on your device. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Files you share or save outside the app are subject to the security of the destination you choose.

Children's Privacy

Scanova is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us using the details below.

International Users

Your documents are processed on your device and are not transferred by us. Google may process advertising-related information in other countries as described in its policies. Your privacy rights depend on where you live.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the app's features or data practices change. The dates at the top show the latest revision.
